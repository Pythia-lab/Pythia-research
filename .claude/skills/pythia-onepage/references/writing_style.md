# Writing style

The voice is a skeptical cash-flow analyst writing for an investor with 60 seconds. Every sentence either states a
number with its comparison or a judgement the numbers support. No adjectives without numbers, no "we believe", no
hedging filler, no buy/sell recommendation. The valuation paragraph states conditions, not price targets.

## Core moves
1. **Verdict headlines.** Commentary opens with a bold 3–7 word claim ending in a full stop — a conclusion, not a
   topic. From the references: "Cash grows; reinvestment takes the gain." · "Search is the central engine." ·
   "Cloud profit is one test." · "Demand must become collectible cash." · "Judge the benefit per share." ·
   "The price needs a cash case." · "Expansion masked weak demand." · "Traffic has yet to restore margins." ·
   "New stores need time." · "Test new-store payback." · "Leases change the return test." · "Fewer shares lifted
   EPS." · "Smaller fleet, firmer demand." · "Conversion is the bottleneck." · "Strip out one-offs." · "Capital
   shapes fees." · "Negative ROE: negative equity." · "Test per-share cash."
   Patterns: *X drives/masks Y* · *X needs Y* · *X is (not) Y* · *Test X* · *Strip out X* · *X changes the test*.
2. **Numbers with their comparison.** "store margin fell from 24.5% to 23.3%", "FCF/sh +2.4% vs total +0.7%".
3. **Write out the identity.** "OCF $39.4B up; cash PP&E up $38.9B; FCF +0.7%." "FY11-25 OCF $14.16B less cash
   PP&E $5.27B yielded $8.89B FCF."
4. **Separate what helped from what lasts.** "Lower TAC reflects the Network mix shift, not better Search terms."
5. **Refuse false credit / double counting.** "Commitments are not cash or capital returns." "Backlog is not
   collected revenue." "SBC is a real cost excluded from reported FCF."
6. **End with the test.** The last left-column paragraph and the RETURN CONDITION sidebar say what must happen.
7. **Scope honesty.** Flag distorted ratios (negative equity, lease treatment, one-offs) and footnote the detail.

## The valuation paragraph (paragraph 6, PYTHIA addition)
State (a) the base value and its two key inputs, (b) what operating path it requires, (c) what today's price
implies, (d) the gap to Morningstar/S&P and its main driver. Example:
"**The DCF asks for traffic, not hope.** Base value $41 (8.5% WACC, 3% g) needs FCF/sh to compound ~12% to FY30 at
flat margins. At $36.64 the market prices ~9%. Mine sits 6.8% below Morningstar, mostly on slower unit growth."
ZH: "**DCF 要的是客流，不是希望。** 基准价值 $41（WACC 8.5%，g 3%）要求每股FCF 至 FY30 年化增长约 12%……"

## Length budget (fits the page; the renderer's hints tell you to add/cut)
| Block | EN | ZH |
|---|---|---|
| Business chain | 5 lines × 8–14 words | 5 行 × 18–30 字 |
| Watch line | ≤ 12 words | ≤ 25 字 |
| Sidebar | 4–6 blocks × 15–30 words; final block 20–35 | 每块 30–60 字 |
| CAPITAL RETURNS / NET SHARES | 45–60 words each | 各 80–110 字 |
| Commentary | 6 × 35–50 words (≈ 240–290 total) | 6 × 60–90 字 |
| Footnotes | 5–7 lines ≤ 25 words | 5–7 行 |

## Mechanics
- Abbreviate consistently: Rev., Op., OCF, FCF, PP&E, SBC, YoY, bp, sh, comps, TAC, AUV, TTM, 9M26, FY11-25.
- `$B` in prose for billions (two decimals: `$2.44B`), `$M` in tables, one decimal for %.
- `->` for chains, `;` to pack facts, `vs` for comparisons, `~` for approximations.
- Mark subsequent events explicitly (`Q3 2026`, `9M26`) so the FY25 base stays clean.

## Chinese version (中文版)
- Same structure and same numbers; prose fully Chinese, metric short names may stay English (OCF, FCF, ROIC, P/E).
- 结论句加粗、以句号结尾：「现金增长，增量被再投资吸收。」「搜索是核心引擎。」「需求必须转为可回收的现金。」
  「衡量每股受益。」「价格需要现金支撑。」「开店增长掩盖了客流问题。」
- 用全角标点；数字与单位不拆开（$1.3M、25.4%）；「较」「由…降至…」「占…」表达对比。
- Do not translate word-for-word from English; rewrite in natural analyst Chinese with the same claims.
- If the user's analysis is in Chinese, keep their judgements; the English version restates them in the house voice.
